About Our Business

More Than Just a Masala Store

Suresh Kirana Store is a local grocery shop located in the main market of Kajra Bazar, Bihar. Our physical store offers a variety of everyday grocery and household essentials.

Through this website, we bring special attention to four selected products: Haldi Powder, Red Mirchi Powder, Dhaniya Powder and Pure Sarso Oil.

Our aim is to make these selected products accessible to customers in Kajra and nearby areas.
