# 🌶️ Suresh Masala – Authentic Taste of Kajra

**A Freshly Ground Masala Mill | 10+ Years of Trust, Quality & Authentic Taste**

Suresh Masala is a local spice mill based in Kajra, Bihar, dedicated to delivering fresh, quality spices with authentic traditional flavours. For over 10 years, we have been serving customers in Kajra and nearby villages with freshness, purity, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🌿 About the Project

The Suresh Masala website is a responsive business website designed to showcase the brand, its products, services, and commitment to quality.

The website aims to establish an online presence for a traditional local masala business and make it easier for customers to explore its offerings.

## ✨ Features

- Modern and clean website design
- Fully responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Attractive homepage and hero section
- About Us section highlighting the brand's journey
- Products and services showcase
- High-quality images and visual presentation
- Smooth navigation and interactive UI
- Customer-focused business information
