From Farm to Kitchen

Our Fresh Masala Process

Suresh Masala has been serving Kajra and nearby villages for over 10 years. We carefully clean dry red chillies, mustard seeds and coriander before grinding them fresh in our own mill. Our authentic taste and quality have earned the love and trust of local customers. Your trust inspires us to serve you better every day.

STEP 01

Sun Drying & Preparation

Selected spices like coriander and red chilli are carefully cleaned and dried in sunlight to prepare them for grinding.

STEP 02

Fresh Machine Grinding

After preparation, the spices are ground in a machine to create fresh, aromatic masala for your kitchen.
